KindredCube Cookie Policy

Effective Date: August 16, 2026 Last Updated: August 16, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how KindredCube may use cookies and similar technologies on our website and related web pages to keep the service working, improve security, remember choices, measure performance, and support compliance.

1. Introduction

KindredCube is a dating and social discovery platform developed by Tectavis, Inc. Our website provides information about the KindredCube mobile application, legal policies, support information, download information, compatibility insights, and related resources.

This Cookie Policy should be read together with our Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, and Community Guidelines. It applies to the KindredCube website and any related web pages that link to this Cookie Policy.

2. What Cookies Are

Cookies are small text files that a website may store on your browser or device. Cookies can help a website remember information about your visit, such as your preferences, session state, language choices, security signals, or how you interact with a page.

Similar technologies may include local storage, session storage, pixels, tags, scripts, software development kits, browser identifiers, device identifiers, and other technologies that store or read information on a browser or device.

3. Why KindredCube Uses Cookies

KindredCube may use cookies and similar technologies for purposes that include operating the website, protecting the website, maintaining secure sessions, remembering preferences, measuring website performance, improving page design, understanding which resources are useful, supporting legal compliance, preventing abuse, and assisting customer support.

We do not use cookies to sell personal information. We also do not use cookies to track users across unrelated third-party apps or websites for third-party advertising purposes.

4. Categories of Cookies We May Use

4.1 Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are required for the website to function. They may be used for security, page routing, load balancing, form protection, session handling, legal page delivery, and basic website operation. Without these cookies, parts of the website may not work properly.

4.2 Security and Fraud Prevention Cookies

Security cookies help us detect suspicious traffic, automated requests, abuse attempts, unauthorized access attempts, and technical issues. They may also support rate limiting, bot detection, and protection of admin or support features.

4.3 Preference Cookies

Preference cookies may remember choices such as language, display preferences, region, consent choices, or whether you have already seen a particular notice. These cookies help make the website easier to use.

4.4 Analytics and Performance Cookies

Analytics cookies may help us understand how visitors use the website, which pages are visited, how long pages take to load, where errors occur, and how we can improve the user experience. Analytics may be aggregated or de-identified where possible.

4.5 Support and Communication Cookies

If support tools, forms, or help pages are used, cookies or similar technologies may help route support requests, protect forms from abuse, remember support session context, or measure whether help content is useful.

5. Third-Party Services

KindredCube may use trusted service providers to support website hosting, security, analytics, payments, email delivery, support, maps, infrastructure, and performance monitoring. These providers may use cookies or similar technologies when necessary to provide their services.

Third-party providers may include hosting providers, security providers, payment processors, email providers, analytics providers, and infrastructure providers. Their use of cookies and related technologies may be governed by their own policies.

6. Cookies and the KindredCube Mobile App

The KindredCube mobile application may use device storage, secure storage, push notification tokens, authentication tokens, and similar technologies to keep users signed in, deliver messages, support profile features, cache images, and improve performance. These technologies are described in more detail in our Privacy Policy.

This Cookie Policy focuses mainly on the website, but similar storage technologies may support app functionality where appropriate.

7. Legal Pages and Admin Updates

KindredCube legal pages may be served from static website content or from approved admin-managed legal content. Cookies or local storage may help the website load pages efficiently or remember security-related settings. Legal pages should remain accessible even where non-essential cookies are disabled.

8. Your Choices

You can usually control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to block cookies, delete cookies, or receive warnings before cookies are stored. Your device or browser may also provide settings to limit tracking technologies.

If you block or delete cookies, some website features may not work correctly. Essential cookies may still be used where necessary to provide the website, protect security, or comply with legal obligations.

9. Consent Where Required

Where required by applicable law, KindredCube will request consent before using non-essential cookies or similar technologies. You may be able to withdraw or adjust cookie choices through browser settings, device settings, or cookie controls provided on the website where available.

10. Data Retention

Cookies may remain on your device for different periods. Session cookies are generally deleted when you close your browser. Persistent cookies may remain until they expire or until you delete them. The retention period depends on the purpose of the cookie and the settings of the relevant provider.

11. Updates to This Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our website, mobile app, providers, legal requirements, or security practices. The "Last Updated" date shows when this version became effective.
