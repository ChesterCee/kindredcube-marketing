What cookies are

Cookies are small files stored on your browser or device. Similar technologies may include local storage, pixels, tags, and device identifiers used by websites and service providers.

How KindredCube may use cookies

  • Essential functionality: to keep pages working, remember basic preferences, and support secure website sessions.
  • Security and fraud prevention: to detect suspicious activity, abuse, automated traffic, or unauthorized access attempts.
  • Analytics: to understand which pages are visited, how visitors use the website, and how we can improve performance and usability.
  • Support and compliance: to help with customer support, legal pages, cookie choices, and documentation updates.

Third-party services

Your choices
