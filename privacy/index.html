KindredCube Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 8-01-2026Last Updated: 08-01-2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to KindredCube ("KindredCube," "we," "our," or "us"), a social discovery and dating platform developed by Tectavis, Inc.

KindredCube is designed to help adults discover compatible people through profile matching, location-based discovery, messaging, identity verification, and optional features that encourage safe and meaningful real-world connections.

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains:

  • What information we collect.
  • Why we collect it.
  • How we use it.
  • When we share it.
  • How we protect it.
  • Your rights regarding your personal information.

By creating an account or using KindredCube, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

2. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to:

  • The KindredCube mobile application.
  • The KindredCube website.
  • Customer support services.
  • Identity verification services provided through our platform.
  • Features such as Connect, Ready to Meet, Global Connect, messaging, profile verification, and safety tools.

This Privacy Policy does not apply to third-party websites, applications, or services that operate under their own privacy policies, even if they are accessible through KindredCube.

3. Eligibility

KindredCube is intended only for adults.

You must be at least 18 years old to create an account or use the Service.

We do not knowingly permit children to create accounts. If we become aware that an account belongs to a person under the required minimum age, we may suspend or permanently delete the account.

4. Definitions

Account means the registered user profile used to access KindredCube.

Personal Information means information that identifies or can reasonably be linked to an individual.

Profile Information means information you choose to display on your profile, including photographs, biography, interests, lifestyle information, and relationship preferences.

Verification Information means information used to confirm the authenticity of an account or the identity of a user.

Ready to Meet means an optional feature that allows users to temporarily indicate they are available to meet other users nearby.

Global Connect means an optional feature that allows users to discover and be discovered by people in selected countries or regions outside their normal location.

Service means the KindredCube application, website, and related services.

5. Information We Collect

The information we collect depends on how you use KindredCube and which features you choose to enable.

5.1 Account Information

When you register for KindredCube, we collect information necessary to create and manage your account.

This may include:

  • Full name
  • Username
  • Email address
  • Phone number (where provided or required)
  • Date of birth
  • Age confirmation
  • Gender
  • Relationship preferences
  • Password (stored only as a secure cryptographic hash)
  • Email verification status
  • Account status
  • Login history
  • Authentication and session information

We use this information to:

  • Create your account.
  • Authenticate your identity.
  • Secure your account.
  • Prevent unauthorized access.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Maintain the integrity of our platform.

5.2 Profile Information

Your profile allows other users to understand who you are and what type of connection you are seeking.

Depending on the information you choose to provide, your profile may include:

  • Profile photographs
  • Biography
  • Interests
  • Hobbies
  • Occupation
  • Education
  • Languages spoken
  • Height
  • Lifestyle preferences
  • Relationship goals
  • Personality prompts and responses
  • Community or cultural preferences
  • Search preferences
  • Other profile information voluntarily provided by you

Most profile information is optional unless specifically identified as required during registration.

Information that you choose to make public may be visible to other users in accordance with your privacy settings.

5.3 Identity Verification Information

To improve authenticity, reduce fraud, and promote user safety, KindredCube may offer identity or profile verification.

Depending on the verification method used, we may collect:

  • Verification selfie
  • Profile photograph used for comparison
  • Verification status
  • Verification timestamps
  • Verification provider
  • Verification outcome
  • Verification badge status

Identity verification may be performed using trusted third-party providers.

Where facial comparison technology is used, verification images are processed solely for identity verification, fraud prevention, duplicate-account detection, and community safety.

Verification badges displayed on profiles indicate only that a verification process has been completed. They do not guarantee a person's identity, character, intentions, or future behaviour.

5.4 Location Information

Location information is an important part of several KindredCube features.

With your permission, we may collect:

  • Approximate location
  • City
  • Region or state
  • Country
  • Approximate distance from other users
  • Precise GPS location where required for specific features and permitted by your device settings

Location information may be used for:

  • Connect
  • Nearby discovery
  • Ready to Meet
  • Global Connect
  • Matching recommendations
  • Fraud prevention
  • Community safety
  • Abuse detection

KindredCube is designed to protect location privacy.

Unless you explicitly choose otherwise, we do not publicly display your precise GPS coordinates, residential address, workplace, or exact real-time location.

Instead, users are generally shown approximate distance, city, region, or other limited location information appropriate to the feature being used.

You may disable location permissions through your device settings at any time. Certain location-based features may become unavailable if location access is disabled.

5.5 Information You Choose to Provide

You may voluntarily provide additional information while using KindredCube.

Examples include:

  • Responses to profile prompts
  • Customer support requests
  • Feedback submitted to us
  • Survey responses
  • Safety reports
  • Bug reports
  • Feature suggestions
  • Contest or promotional entries
  • Other information you choose to share with us

Providing optional information is voluntary. Choosing not to provide optional information may limit access to certain features but will not prevent you from using the core functionality of the Service unless the information is required for safety or operational purposes.

5.6 Accuracy of Information

You are responsible for ensuring that the information you provide is accurate and up to date.

Providing false, misleading, or fraudulent information may result in verification failure, feature restrictions, suspension, or permanent removal from KindredCube in accordance with our Terms of Service.
