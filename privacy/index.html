KindredCube Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 1, 2026 Last Updated: August 15, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to KindredCube ("KindredCube," "we," "our," or "us"), a social discovery and dating platform developed by Tectavis, Inc.

KindredCube is designed to help adults discover compatible people through profile matching, location-based discovery, messaging, identity verification, optional paid features, and safety tools that encourage meaningful real-world connections.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, when we share it, how we protect it, how long we keep it, and the choices and rights you may have regarding your information.

By creating an account or using KindredCube, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

2. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the KindredCube mobile application, the KindredCube website, customer support services, identity and selfie verification features, and product features including Connect, Explore, Ready to Meet, messaging, profile verification, Wallet, KindredPass, Premium, reporting, blocking, and safety tools.

This Privacy Policy does not apply to third-party websites, applications, or services that operate under their own privacy policies, even if they are accessible through KindredCube.

3. Eligibility

KindredCube is intended only for adults. You must be at least 18 years old to create an account or use the Service.

We do not knowingly permit children to create accounts. If we become aware that an account belongs to a person under the required minimum age, we may suspend or permanently delete the account.

4. Definitions

Account means the registered user profile used to access KindredCube.

Personal Information means information that identifies or can reasonably be linked to an individual.

Profile Information means information you choose to display on your profile, including photographs, biography, interests, lifestyle information, prompts, relationship preferences, and compatibility information.

Verification Information means information used to confirm the authenticity of an account, verify a user, reduce duplicate accounts, or support trust and safety decisions.

Ready to Meet means an optional feature that allows users to temporarily indicate that they are available to meet other eligible users nearby.

Service means the KindredCube application, website, and related services.

5. Information We Collect

The information we collect depends on how you use KindredCube and which features you choose to enable.

5.1 Account Information

When you register for KindredCube, we collect information necessary to create, protect, and manage your account. This may include your full name, username, email address, phone number where provided or required, date of birth, age confirmation, gender, relationship preferences, password, email verification status, account status, login history, authentication information, and session information.

Passwords are never stored in plain text. Passwords are stored only as secure cryptographic hashes with additional security protections.

We use this information to create your account, authenticate your identity, secure your account, prevent unauthorized access, provide customer support, and maintain platform integrity.

5.2 Profile Information

Your profile allows other users to understand who you are and what type of connection you are seeking. Depending on the information you choose to provide, your profile may include profile photos, biography, interests, hobbies, occupation, education, languages spoken, height, lifestyle preferences, relationship goals, personality type, Kindred Type responses, profile prompts, values, causes, communities, children preferences, religion, politics, smoking, drinking, cannabis, and other optional details.

Most profile information is optional unless specifically identified as required during registration or necessary for a specific feature. Information that you choose to make public may be visible to other users in accordance with app functionality and your settings.

5.3 Kindred Type and Compatibility Information

KindredCube may invite users to answer private compatibility statements about values, faith and spirituality, relationships, ethics, conflict style, lifestyle, ambition, politics, and society. These answers are used to calculate compatibility and improve matching.

Individual Kindred Type responses are private and are not shown publicly. Public profiles may show an overall compatibility score or compatibility signal, but not the individual answers behind that score.

5.4 Identity and Selfie Verification Information

To improve authenticity, reduce fraud, prevent duplicate accounts, and promote user safety, KindredCube may offer identity or profile verification.

Depending on the verification method used, we may collect or process verification selfies, profile photos used for comparison, pose or liveness checks, verification status, verification timestamps, verification provider, verification outcome, verification badge status, and fraud-prevention signals.

Stripe Identity may process government ID verification. KindredCube does not store government identification documents submitted to Stripe.

Selfie verification and duplicate-account checks may use Amazon Rekognition or another trusted face comparison provider. Where Amazon Rekognition is used, verification photos may be sent to Amazon Rekognition for face comparison, liveness-related checks, duplicate-account detection, and safety enforcement. KindredCube may retain verification results, timestamps, provider identifiers, match results, audit records, and limited security metadata needed to prevent fraud, duplicate accounts, banned-user re-entry, and abuse. KindredCube does not publicly display verification selfies or government identification documents.

Verification badges indicate that a verification process has been completed. They do not guarantee a person's identity, character, intentions, compatibility, honesty, or future behaviour.

5.5 Location Information

With your permission, we may collect approximate location, city, region or state, country, approximate distance from other users, and precise GPS location where required for specific features and permitted by your device settings.

Location information may be used for Connect, nearby discovery, Explore recommendations, Ready to Meet, matching recommendations, fraud prevention, community safety, and abuse detection.

KindredCube is designed to protect location privacy. Unless you explicitly choose otherwise, we do not publicly display your precise GPS coordinates, residential address, workplace, or exact real-time location. Users are generally shown approximate distance, city, region, or other limited location information appropriate to the feature being used.

You may disable location permissions through your device settings at any time. Certain location-based features may become unavailable if location access is disabled.

5.6 Messages and Communications

KindredCube provides communication features that allow users to interact after establishing a connection, match, paid access, or permitted interaction.

Depending on the features you use, we may collect and process direct messages, message timestamps, delivery and read status, images shared through chat, voice messages where available, GIFs, stickers, photo comments, message reactions, meeting invitations, meeting responses, customer support communications, safety reports, block and report reasons, and moderation communications.

Messages are intended to be private between participants. However, we may access, preserve, or review message content where reasonably necessary to investigate abuse reports, protect user safety, detect fraud or illegal activity, comply with applicable law, respond to valid legal requests, or enforce our Terms and Community Guidelines.

5.7 Photos, Camera, Microphone, and Media

KindredCube allows users to upload or capture media to personalize their profile and communicate with other users. Depending on how you use the Service, we may process profile photographs, images shared in chat, verification selfies, camera images, voice recordings, and media metadata required for processing uploads.

Access to your camera, photo library, or microphone is requested only when you choose to use a feature that requires those permissions. You may revoke these permissions through your device settings at any time.

5.8 Device and Technical Information

To operate, secure, and improve KindredCube, we automatically collect certain technical information from your device. This may include device model, operating system, operating system version, application version, IP address, device language, time zone, push notification token, security-related device identifiers, session identifiers, browser type where applicable, network information, crash reports, error logs, diagnostic information, and security logs.

We use this information to authenticate devices, maintain secure sessions, detect suspicious activity, prevent fraud, troubleshoot technical issues, improve application stability, deliver push notifications, and monitor platform performance.

5.9 Usage Information

We collect information about how users interact with KindredCube to improve matching, recommendations, platform performance, and community safety. Usage information may include profiles viewed, likes, passes, matches, searches, filters, discovery preferences, time spent using features, Ready to Meet activity, verification activity, notification preferences, profile completion progress, safety feature usage, and payment feature usage.

We do not use your activity to track you across unrelated third-party applications or websites for third-party advertising purposes.

5.10 Payment Information

KindredCube may offer optional paid subscriptions, Wallet top-ups, KindredPass, Premium, Ready to Meet access, photo comments, Super Likes, and other paid features.

Payment transactions may be processed by authorized payment providers, including Stripe, Apple, Google, or other platform payment systems where required. KindredCube does not store your complete payment card information.

Depending on the payment method used, we may receive subscription status, purchase history, billing country where available, transaction reference numbers, product purchased, renewal status, refund status, payment confirmation, Wallet balance, and Wallet transaction history. We use this information to activate paid features, manage subscriptions, process renewals, detect fraudulent transactions, provide customer support, and comply with accounting and legal obligations.

6. How We Use Your Information

KindredCube uses personal information where there is a legitimate reason to do so, including to create and maintain accounts, verify email addresses, authenticate users, recover account access, secure accounts, display profiles, recommend compatible users, deliver messaging, enable Connect, enable Ready to Meet, process verification, provide support, process purchases, personalize the user experience, prevent fraud, detect fake or duplicate accounts, investigate reports, enforce Community Guidelines, send notifications, improve the Service, and comply with legal obligations.

Where permitted by law and your communication preferences, we may send information about new features, product updates, safety notices, support responses, or marketing communications. You may opt out of marketing communications without affecting essential service communications.

7. Ready to Meet

Ready to Meet is an optional feature that allows users to temporarily indicate they are available to meet other users in person. Participation is voluntary.

When you enable Ready to Meet, your profile may become visible to eligible users based on location, preferences, safety settings, access rules, and availability windows. Information that may be displayed includes profile photo, display name, age, verification badges, approximate distance or nearby area, and availability status.

Ready to Meet does not publicly display your exact GPS coordinates, residential address, workplace, or precise real-time location. Users may turn Ready to Meet on or off, set an availability window, limit visibility based on preferences, and stop appearing when the selected availability period expires.

For community safety, KindredCube may require verification, payment, account integrity checks, or other eligibility requirements before allowing access to Ready to Meet or meetup proposals.

8. Global Connect and International Discovery

Where available, Global Connect or international discovery features may allow users to discover and be discovered by people outside their current country or region. Users may choose the countries or regions in which they would like their profile to appear.

International discovery may use selected country or region, discovery preferences, relationship goals, language preferences, profile visibility settings, and compatibility preferences. These features do not disclose your exact physical location.

9. Matching, Recommendations, and Automated Systems

KindredCube uses automated systems to improve compatibility recommendations and the overall user experience. Recommendation systems may consider relationship preferences, preferred age range, distance preferences, interests, lifestyle information, languages, profile completeness, verification status, shared interests, activity within the Service, safety signals, Kindred Type compatibility, previous interactions, and search preferences.

Automated recommendations assist discovery but do not determine who you must communicate with or meet. Users remain in control of their interactions and decisions.

KindredCube does not use automated matching to infer sensitive characteristics that users have not voluntarily provided.

10. Verification Badges

Verification badges help users understand which verification processes have been completed by another user. Depending on available features, badges may include Selfie Verified, ID Verified, Meetup Verified, and other verification badges introduced in future updates.

Selfie verification and ID verification may be displayed differently. ID verification may override selfie verification where both are completed. Meetup Verified may indicate that a user has completed a successful post-meet process according to KindredCube rules.

Verification badges are intended to increase transparency and confidence within the community. They should not be interpreted as a guarantee of safety, honesty, compatibility, or future behaviour.

11. Post-Meet Feedback and Internal Safety Signals

Following an in-person meeting arranged through KindredCube, we may invite participants to complete confidential post-meet feedback. Questions may relate to whether the other person attended, whether the profile accurately represented the individual, whether the user felt safe, whether the person behaved respectfully, and whether the user would be comfortable meeting again.

Individual responses are private and are not published on profiles. KindredCube does not display public ratings, public reputation scores, or public reviews based on post-meet feedback.

KindredCube may use confidential trust and safety signals to improve recommendations, reduce fraudulent activity, limit access to certain features, trigger additional verification, prioritize accounts for manual review, and enforce Community Guidelines. Internal safety scores or signals are confidential and are used exclusively to support platform integrity and community safety.

12. How We Share Information

KindredCube does not sell your personal information. We share personal information only when necessary to operate the Service, comply with legal obligations, protect users, or where you have instructed or authorized us to do so.

Depending on the features you use, information may be shared with service providers that support cloud hosting, database hosting, file and media storage, identity verification, face verification or fraud prevention, payment processing, email delivery, push notifications, maps, customer support, analytics, crash reporting, security monitoring, and infrastructure management.

We may disclose personal information if reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to lawful requests, enforce our Terms, investigate fraud or criminal activity, protect rights and safety, prevent imminent harm, or complete a business transaction such as a merger, acquisition, financing, restructuring, or sale of assets.

13. Apple App Privacy and Google Play Data Safety

KindredCube may collect data categories that include Contact Info, Location, User Content, Photos and Videos, Audio, Identifiers, Purchases, Usage Data, Diagnostics, Sensitive Information voluntarily provided in profiles or reports, camera access, microphone access, and device information.

Data may be linked to your identity and used for app functionality, personalization, fraud prevention, security, customer support, analytics, notifications, developer communications, purchases, legal compliance, and platform safety.

Data is encrypted in transit where technically supported. Users may request account deletion and data deletion through the app or by contacting support, subject to legal, safety, fraud-prevention, accounting, and dispute-resolution retention requirements.

14. International Data Transfers

KindredCube may process or store personal information in countries other than the country in which you reside. When personal information is transferred internationally, we implement appropriate safeguards designed to protect your information in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

15. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Service, maintain account security, prevent fraud, resolve disputes, enforce our Terms, comply with legal obligations, and protect users.

Account and profile information is generally retained while your account remains active. You may update or remove profile information through the application.

Messages may be retained to support messaging functionality, abuse investigations, moderation, legal compliance, and dispute resolution. Where messages are deleted by users, copies may remain for a limited period in backups or where retention is required for safety or legal reasons.

Verification status, timestamps, provider identifiers, audit information, and related security records may be retained to maintain account integrity, prevent fraud, detect duplicate accounts, and enforce community safety. Safety reports, moderation decisions, appeals, and related evidence may be retained for longer periods where reasonably necessary to protect users or enforce our Community Guidelines.

16. Data Security

KindredCube implements administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. These measures may include encryption in transit, encryption of sensitive information where appropriate, password hashing, multi-factor authentication for administrative access, access controls, security monitoring, audit logging, rate limiting, fraud detection, secure development practices, and regular security reviews.

No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should also take reasonable steps to protect their own accounts, including choosing strong passwords and keeping login credentials confidential.

17. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The KindredCube website and related online services may use cookies and similar technologies to maintain user sessions, remember preferences, improve website functionality, measure website performance, analyze usage trends, and enhance security. Where required by law, users will be given appropriate choices regarding non-essential cookies.

18. Government and Law Enforcement Requests

KindredCube reviews requests from government agencies and law enforcement authorities to ensure they are legally valid before disclosing personal information. Where permitted by law, we may notify affected users before disclosing their information. We may decline requests that we believe are unlawful, overly broad, or inconsistent with applicable legal requirements.

19. Protecting Our Community

KindredCube may investigate suspected violations of our Terms of Service or Community Guidelines. Where necessary to protect users and the integrity of the platform, we may review reports of misconduct, investigate suspected fraud, investigate fake accounts, detect spam and scams, restrict access to features, suspend or terminate accounts, and preserve relevant evidence for legal or safety purposes.

20. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and the laws that apply to you, you may have rights to access personal information, correct inaccurate information, update your account, download or receive a copy of certain information, request deletion, object to or restrict certain processing, withdraw consent, or lodge a complaint with a data protection authority.

Certain rights may be limited where we are legally required or permitted to retain information for fraud prevention, security, legal compliance, dispute resolution, accounting, or enforcement of our Terms and Community Guidelines.

21. Account Deletion

You may permanently delete your KindredCube account through the application's account settings where available, or by contacting support.

When your account is deleted, your public profile will no longer be visible to other users, your profile photographs will be removed from public display, active subscriptions may need to be managed through the applicable payment provider or app store, and your access to the Service will end.

Following account deletion, we will delete or anonymize personal information within a reasonable period unless retention is required for legal obligations, fraud prevention, safety investigations, Community Guidelines enforcement, dispute resolution, financial record-keeping, protection of other users, or exercise or defense of legal claims.

22. Children's Privacy

KindredCube is intended exclusively for individuals who are at least eighteen (18) years old. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children or permit individuals under 18 to use the Service.

If we become aware that an account belongs to a person under the required minimum age, we may suspend the account, remove associated content, delete the account, and take any additional steps reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law.

23. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our services, new features, changes in applicable law, improvements to privacy and security practices, or changes to third-party service providers.

When material changes are made, we may provide notice through in-app notifications, email notifications, updates on our website, or other reasonable methods. The "Last Updated" date indicates when this version became effective.
