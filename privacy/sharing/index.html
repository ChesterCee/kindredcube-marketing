14. Sharing Your Information

KindredCube does not sell your personal information.

We only share personal information when necessary to operate the Service, comply with legal obligations, protect our users, or where you have instructed or authorized us to do so.

Depending on the features you use, we may share information with the following categories of recipients.

14.1 Service Providers

We work with carefully selected third-party service providers that perform services on our behalf.

These providers may assist with:

  • Cloud hosting
  • Database hosting
  • File and media storage
  • Identity verification
  • Face verification
  • Payment processing
  • Email delivery
  • Push notifications
  • Customer support
  • Analytics
  • Crash reporting
  • Security monitoring
  • Infrastructure management

These providers are permitted to use your information only for the purposes of providing services to KindredCube and are required to protect your information in accordance with applicable laws and contractual obligations.

14.2 Payment Providers

Where you purchase subscriptions or other paid features, payment information is processed by authorized payment providers.

KindredCube receives only the information necessary to:

  • Confirm payment
  • Activate purchased features
  • Manage subscriptions
  • Process refunds where applicable
  • Prevent fraudulent transactions
  • Meet financial and legal obligations

KindredCube does not receive or store your complete payment card number.

14.3 Identity Verification Providers

Where identity verification features are offered, verification information may be processed by trusted third-party verification providers.

These providers process verification information only for purposes such as:

  • Identity verification
  • Fraud prevention
  • Duplicate-account detection
  • Community safety
  • Account integrity

Verification providers operate under their own privacy policies and security practices.

14.4 Legal Requirements

We may disclose personal information if we believe disclosure is reasonably necessary to:

  • Comply with applicable law.
  • Respond to lawful requests from courts or government authorities.
  • Enforce our Terms of Service.
  • Investigate fraud or criminal activity.
  • Protect the rights, safety, or property of KindredCube, our users, or others.
  • Prevent imminent harm where permitted by law.

14.5 Business Transfers

If KindredCube is involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, restructuring, sale of assets, or similar business transaction, personal information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

If required by applicable law, users will be notified of any material changes affecting the processing of their personal information.

15. International Data Transfers

KindredCube may process or store personal information in countries other than the country in which you reside.

When personal information is transferred internationally, we implement appropriate safeguards designed to protect your information in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

These safeguards may include:

  • Contractual protections
  • Technical security measures
  • Access controls
  • Encryption where appropriate
  • Other legally recognized transfer mechanisms

16. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to:

  • Provide the Service.
  • Maintain account security.
  • Prevent fraud.
  • Resolve disputes.
  • Enforce our Terms of Service.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

Retention periods vary depending on the type of information involved.

Account Information

Account information is generally retained while your account remains active.

If your account is deleted, certain information may be retained where required for fraud prevention, legal compliance, dispute resolution, or enforcement of platform rules.

Profile Information

Profile information is retained while your account remains active.

You may update or remove profile information at any time through the application.

Messages

Messages may be retained to support messaging functionality, abuse investigations, moderation, legal compliance, and dispute resolution.

Where messages are deleted by users, copies may remain for a limited period in backups or where retention is required for safety or legal reasons.

Verification Information

Verification status, verification timestamps, and related audit information may be retained to maintain account integrity, prevent fraud, detect duplicate accounts, and enforce community safety.

Safety Reports

Reports, moderation decisions, appeals, and related evidence may be retained for longer periods where reasonably necessary to protect users or enforce our Community Guidelines.

17. Data Security

KindredCube implements administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

These measures may include:

  • Encryption of data in transit.
  • Encryption of sensitive information where appropriate.
  • Password hashing.
  • Multi-factor authentication for administrative access.
  • Access controls based on business need.
  • Security monitoring.
  • Audit logging.
  • Rate limiting.
  • Fraud detection systems.
  • Secure development practices.
  • Regular security reviews.

While we strive to protect personal information, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should also take reasonable steps to protect their own accounts, including choosing strong passwords and keeping login credentials confidential.

18. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The KindredCube website and related online services may use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Maintain user sessions.
  • Remember preferences.
  • Improve website functionality.
  • Measure website performance.
  • Analyze usage trends.
  • Enhance security.

Where required by applicable law, users will be given appropriate choices regarding the use of non-essential cookies.

19. Government and Law Enforcement Requests

KindredCube reviews requests from government agencies and law enforcement authorities to ensure they are legally valid before disclosing personal information.

Where permitted by law, we may notify affected users before disclosing their information.

We may decline requests that we believe are unlawful, overly broad, or inconsistent with applicable legal requirements.

20. Protecting Our Community

KindredCube may investigate suspected violations of our Terms of Service or Community Guidelines.

Where necessary to protect users and the integrity of the platform, we may:

  • Review reports of misconduct.
  • Investigate suspected fraud.
  • Investigate fake accounts.
  • Detect spam and scams.
  • Restrict access to certain features.
  • Suspend or terminate accounts.
  • Preserve relevant evidence for legal or safety purposes.

These actions are intended to protect our community while respecting applicable legal rights and due process where appropriate.
