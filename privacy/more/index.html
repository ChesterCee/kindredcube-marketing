7. Ready to Meet

Ready to Meet is an optional feature that allows users to temporarily indicate they are available to meet other users in person.

Participation in Ready to Meet is entirely voluntary.

When you enable Ready to Meet, your profile may become visible to eligible users based on your location, preferences, and applicable safety settings.

Information that may be displayed includes:

  • Profile photo
  • First name or display name
  • Age
  • Verification badges
  • Approximate distance or nearby area
  • Availability status

Ready to Meet is designed with privacy in mind.

KindredCube does not publicly display your exact GPS coordinates, residential address, workplace, or precise real-time location through this feature.

Users may:

  • Turn Ready to Meet on or off at any time.
  • Set an availability window.
  • Limit visibility based on their preferences.
  • Stop appearing when the selected availability period expires.

For community safety, KindredCube may require certain verification or account integrity requirements before allowing access to Ready to Meet.

8. Global Connect

Global Connect is an optional feature that allows users to discover and be discovered by people outside their current country or region.

Users choose the countries or regions in which they would like their profile to appear.

Global Connect may use:

  • Selected country or region
  • Discovery preferences
  • Relationship goals
  • Language preferences
  • Profile visibility settings
  • Compatibility preferences

Participation in Global Connect is optional.

Turning off Global Connect immediately removes your profile from international discovery, subject to normal system processing times.

Global Connect does not disclose your exact physical location. Users remain in control of their visibility preferences.

9. AI Matching and Recommendations

KindredCube uses automated systems to improve compatibility recommendations and the overall user experience.

Our recommendation systems may consider information including:

  • Relationship preferences
  • Preferred age range
  • Distance preferences
  • Global Connect preferences
  • Interests
  • Lifestyle information
  • Languages
  • Profile completeness
  • Verification status
  • Shared interests
  • Activity within the Service
  • Safety signals
  • Previous interactions
  • Search preferences

These systems are intended to improve the quality and relevance of recommendations.

Automated recommendations assist discovery but do not determine who you must communicate with or meet. Users remain in full control of their interactions and decisions.

KindredCube does not use automated matching to infer sensitive characteristics that users have not voluntarily provided.

10. Face Verification

KindredCube may provide identity and facial verification features to improve trust, reduce fraud, and help maintain a safer community.

Where enabled, verification may involve comparing a verification selfie with one or more profile photographs.

Verification services may be provided by trusted third-party providers.

Verification information may be used to:

  • Confirm account authenticity.
  • Reduce fake profiles.
  • Detect duplicate accounts.
  • Prevent fraudulent activity.
  • Enforce Community Guidelines.
  • Improve user trust.

Verification results may include:

  • Verification completed
  • Verification failed
  • Verification pending
  • Verification badge eligibility

Verification badges indicate that a verification process has been completed. They do not guarantee a user's identity, intentions, compatibility, or future conduct.

KindredCube does not publicly display government identification documents or verification images.

11. Verification Badges

Verification badges help users understand which verification processes have been completed by another user.

Depending on available features, badges may include:

  • Selfie Verified
  • ID Verified
  • Meetup Verified
  • Other verification badges introduced in future updates

Verification badges are intended to increase transparency and confidence within the community.

They should not be interpreted as a guarantee of safety, honesty, compatibility, or future behaviour.

Users should always exercise appropriate caution when interacting with others both online and in person.

12. Post-Meet Safety Feedback

Following an in-person meeting, KindredCube may invite both participants to complete a confidential post-meet safety questionnaire.

Participation is voluntary.

Questions may relate to topics such as:

  • Whether the other person attended the meeting.
  • Whether the profile accurately represented the individual.
  • Whether the user felt safe.
  • Whether the individual behaved respectfully.
  • Whether the user would be comfortable meeting the person again.

Individual responses remain confidential and are never published on user profiles.

KindredCube does not display public ratings, public reputation scores, or public reviews based on post-meet feedback.

13. Internal Trust and Safety System

To help maintain a safe community, KindredCube may use confidential trust and safety signals generated from multiple sources.

These signals may include:

  • Verification status.
  • Account integrity.
  • Confirmed reports of misconduct.
  • Verified policy violations.
  • Repeated post-meet safety feedback.
  • Fraud indicators.
  • Spam detection.
  • Account authenticity signals.

These signals may be used to:

  • Improve recommendations.
  • Reduce fraudulent activity.
  • Limit access to certain features.
  • Trigger additional verification.
  • Prioritize accounts for manual review.
  • Enforce Community Guidelines.

KindredCube does not rely solely on automated systems when making significant account enforcement decisions.

Where appropriate, decisions affecting account access may be reviewed by authorized personnel.

Users who believe enforcement action has been taken in error may contact KindredCube Support to request a review in accordance with our Terms of Service.

Internal trust and safety scores are confidential, are not visible to other users, and are used exclusively to support platform integrity and community safety.
