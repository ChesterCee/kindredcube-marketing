5.7 Messages and Communications

KindredCube provides communication features that allow users to interact after establishing a connection or match.

Depending on the features you use, we may collect and process:

  • Direct messages
  • Message timestamps
  • Delivery and read status
  • Images shared through chat
  • Voice messages (where available)
  • GIFs and stickers
  • Photo comments
  • Message reactions
  • Meeting invitations and responses
  • Customer support communications
  • Safety reports
  • Block and report reasons

We use this information to:

  • Deliver messaging services.
  • Synchronize conversations across your devices.
  • Improve reliability and performance.
  • Detect spam, scams, fraud, harassment, and other abusive behaviour.
  • Investigate reports and enforce our Community Guidelines.
  • Provide customer support.

Messages are intended to be private between participants. However, we may access, preserve, or review message content where reasonably necessary to:

  • Investigate abuse reports.
  • Protect the safety of users.
  • Detect fraud or illegal activity.
  • Comply with applicable law.
  • Respond to valid legal requests.

5.8 Photos, Camera, Microphone and Media

KindredCube allows users to upload or capture media to personalize their profile and communicate with other users.

Depending on how you use the Service, we may process:

  • Profile photographs.
  • Images shared in chat.
  • Verification selfies.
  • Camera images.
  • Voice recordings.
  • Media metadata required for processing uploads.

Access to your camera, photo library, or microphone is requested only when you choose to use a feature that requires those permissions.

You may revoke these permissions through your device settings at any time. Certain features may become unavailable if the required permissions are disabled.

5.9 Device and Technical Information

To operate, secure, and improve KindredCube, we automatically collect certain technical information from your device.

This may include:

  • Device model.
  • Operating system.
  • Operating system version.
  • Application version.
  • IP address.
  • Device language.
  • Time zone.
  • Push notification token.
  • Device identifiers used for security.
  • Session identifiers.
  • Browser type (where applicable).
  • Network information.
  • Crash reports.
  • Error logs.
  • Diagnostic information.
  • Security logs.

We use this information to:

  • Authenticate devices.
  • Maintain secure sessions.
  • Detect suspicious activity.
  • Prevent fraud.
  • Troubleshoot technical issues.
  • Improve application stability.
  • Deliver push notifications.
  • Monitor platform performance.

5.10 Usage Information

We collect information about how users interact with KindredCube to improve matching, recommendations, platform performance, and community safety.

Usage information may include:

  • Profiles viewed.
  • Likes.
  • Passes.
  • Matches.
  • Searches.
  • Search filters.
  • Discovery preferences.
  • Time spent using features.
  • Ready to Meet activity.
  • Future Global Connect activity, if the feature is introduced.
  • Verification activity.
  • Notification preferences.
  • Profile completion progress.
  • Safety feature usage.

We use this information to:

  • Improve recommendations.
  • Personalize your experience.
  • Improve search quality.
  • Understand feature usage.
  • Improve performance.
  • Detect abuse and fraudulent behaviour.
  • Enhance user safety.

We do not use your activity to track you across third-party applications or websites for advertising purposes.

5.11 Payment Information

KindredCube may offer optional paid subscriptions, premium features, or other paid services.

Payment transactions may be processed by authorized payment providers, including platform payment systems where required.

KindredCube does not store your complete payment card information.

Depending on the payment method used, we may receive information such as:

  • Subscription status.
  • Purchase history.
  • Billing country.
  • Transaction reference numbers.
  • Product purchased.
  • Renewal status.
  • Refund status.
  • Payment confirmation.

This information is used to:

  • Activate paid features.
  • Manage subscriptions.
  • Process renewals.
  • Detect fraudulent transactions.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Comply with accounting and legal obligations.

Payment providers process payment information in accordance with their own privacy policies and security requirements.

6. How We Use Your Information

KindredCube uses personal information only where there is a legitimate reason to do so.

We may use your information to:

Account Management

  • Create and maintain your account.
  • Verify your email address.
  • Authenticate your identity.
  • Recover access to your account.
  • Secure your account against unauthorized access.

Providing the Service

  • Display your profile.
  • Recommend compatible users.
  • Deliver messaging functionality.
  • Enable Connect.
  • Enable Ready to Meet.
  • Support future Global Connect features if they are introduced.
  • Process profile verification.
  • Provide customer support.

Personalization

We use information you provide to personalize your experience, including:

  • Relationship preferences.
  • Preferred age range.
  • Distance preferences.
  • Interests.
  • Lifestyle preferences.
  • Language preferences.
  • Discovery settings.

Safety and Security

Your information may be used to:

  • Detect fake accounts.
  • Prevent duplicate accounts.
  • Detect fraud.
  • Investigate reports.
  • Enforce Community Guidelines.
  • Improve platform safety.
  • Protect users from abuse.
  • Respond to security incidents.

Communications

We may contact you regarding:

  • Account verification.
  • Security alerts.
  • Password resets.
  • Important service announcements.
  • Subscription management.
  • Customer support.
  • Updates to our legal agreements where required.

Where permitted by law and your communication preferences, we may also send information about new features, promotions, or product updates. You may opt out of marketing communications at any time without affecting essential service communications.

Analytics and Improvement

We use aggregated and, where appropriate, de-identified information to:

  • Improve application performance.
  • Develop new features.
  • Understand usage trends.
  • Diagnose technical issues.
  • Improve matching quality.
  • Enhance user experience.

Legal Compliance

We may use personal information where necessary to:

  • Comply with applicable laws.
  • Respond to lawful requests from public authorities.
  • Protect the rights, safety, and property of KindredCube, our users, and others.
  • Prevent fraud and unlawful activity.
  • Resolve disputes.
  • Enforce our Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.
