Delete your KindredCube account

You can permanently delete your account directly from the KindredCube app.

How to delete your account

  1. Sign in to KindredCube.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Select Data management, then Delete account.
  4. Follow the confirmation steps shown on screen.

What happens next

Your active profile and account data will be removed. Some limited records may be retained when required for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or legal compliance, as described in our Privacy Policy.

If you cannot access your account, use KindredCube Support from the app and ask for help with account deletion. We may need to verify that the account belongs to you before processing the request.
