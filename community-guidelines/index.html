KindredCube Community Guidelines

Effective Date: August 15, 2026 Last Updated: August 15, 2026

1. Our Community Commitment

KindredCube exists to help adults build genuine, respectful, and meaningful relationships. Our community is built on respect, authenticity, safety, and accountability.

Every user is responsible for helping create an environment where people can connect without fear of harassment, deception, exploitation, or abuse.

These Community Guidelines apply to every interaction on KindredCube, including profiles, photographs, prompts, messages, photo comments, voice notes, Ready to Meet, Global Connect, verification, reports, support tickets, and in-person meetups.

2. Be Authentic

Authenticity is fundamental to KindredCube. Users must provide truthful profile information, use their real age, use their own identity, represent relationship intentions honestly, and update profile details when important information changes.

  • Use photographs that accurately represent you.
  • Do not create fake profiles or impersonate another person.
  • Do not use another person's photographs.
  • Do not use deceptive, heavily manipulated, or AI-generated images intended to mislead others.
  • Do not misrepresent your marital status, relationship status, career, qualifications, or achievements.
  • Do not create duplicate accounts to bypass reports, blocks, bans, verification, safety restrictions, or payment rules.

3. Respect Other Users

Treat every member with dignity and respect. Be polite, respect boundaries, accept rejection gracefully, respect privacy, and communicate honestly.

KindredCube does not allow insults, humiliation, threats, intimidation, encouragement of self-harm, hate speech, bullying, harassment, stalking, coercion, manipulation, or persistent unwanted contact.

If someone says no, stops replying, blocks you, declines a meeting, or expresses discomfort, respect that immediately.

4. Zero Tolerance for Exploitation

The following conduct is strictly prohibited and may result in permanent removal and referral to appropriate authorities where required by law:

  • Child sexual abuse material, child exploitation, grooming, or any sexualized content involving minors.
  • Human trafficking, sexual exploitation, prostitution, solicitation, escort services, or commercial sexual activity.
  • Blackmail, extortion, revenge pornography, non-consensual intimate images, physical threats, terrorist content, or promotion of violent crime.

5. No Fraud, Scams, or Financial Exploitation

KindredCube is designed for genuine relationships, not financial gain. Users must not ask other users for money, cryptocurrency, gift cards, bank details, passwords, verification codes, private documents, account access, investments, fake emergency assistance, or financial favors.

Romance scams, phishing, malware, pyramid schemes, counterfeit goods, deceptive links, payment manipulation, and misleading users for financial benefit are prohibited.

6. Sexual Content and Consent

KindredCube supports healthy adult relationships, but users must not send unsolicited explicit content, pornography, sexual images, sexual harassment, or messages that pressure another user into sexual activity.

Consensual romantic conversations between adults are permitted when respectful and compliant with these Guidelines. KindredCube must not be used to facilitate prostitution, sexual exploitation, or adult services.

7. Profiles, Photos, Prompts, and Media

Profile photos should clearly show and accurately represent you. Prompts, bio text, photo comments, videos, voice notes, and other media must be truthful, respectful, lawful, and safe.

Primary profile photos must not include someone else, celebrity images, deceptive AI-generated people, pornographic images, graphic violence, hate symbols, illegal content, or content promoting criminal activity.

KindredCube may remove photos, prompts, messages, or media that violate these Guidelines or create safety, authenticity, or legal concerns.

8. Messaging Rules

Messaging should help people build genuine connections. Introduce yourself respectfully, respect unanswered messages, and end conversations politely.

Do not spam users, send repeated unwanted messages, send unsolicited explicit content, threaten users, manipulate users emotionally, pressure someone to meet, or attempt to circumvent blocks.

Users can report or block conversations at any time.

9. Ready to Meet

Ready to Meet is intended to facilitate voluntary, respectful, public in-person meetings. Users must enable availability voluntarily, meet only by mutual agreement, respect cancellations, and meet in safe public places.

Users must not misrepresent availability, misuse Ready to Meet to stalk users, attempt to discover another person's exact location, pressure another user into meeting, or use the feature for unsafe or deceptive purposes.

KindredCube may restrict access to Ready to Meet based on verification status, Wallet or plan access, distance, safety history, post-meet feedback patterns, repeated policy violations, or other app safety rules.

10. Global Connect

Global Connect helps users discover people in other countries and cultures. Users must not use Global Connect to evade restrictions, misrepresent relocation intentions, mislead users about immigration opportunities, conduct recruitment scams, or exploit cultural or language differences for fraud.

11. Verification and Duplicate Account Prevention

Users participating in Stripe ID verification, selfie verification, or other verification checks must submit their own information and cooperate honestly.

You must not manipulate verification photos, use another person's identity, submit fraudulent documents, use AI-generated identity images, or attempt to bypass duplicate-account detection.

Attempts to bypass verification systems may result in suspension, permanent removal, loss of feature access, or preservation of safety records to protect the community.

12. Post-Meet Safety Feedback

Post-meet feedback helps improve community safety. Users must provide feedback honestly and must not submit false reports, coordinate false reports, retaliate through the feedback system, or attempt to manipulate another person's safety standing.

KindredCube may disregard reports or feedback that appear intentionally false, abusive, retaliatory, or coordinated. Repeated misuse of reporting or feedback systems may result in enforcement action.

13. Reporting, Blocking, and Support

If you encounter behavior that violates these Guidelines, report it using the in-app reporting tools or contact support through the app. Users may also block other users at any time.

Users may report fake profiles, harassment, fraud, hate speech, threats, explicit content, impersonation, spam, dangerous behavior, payment abuse, duplicate accounts, or any other suspected violation.

Support tickets must be truthful and should not be used to harass staff, threaten users, submit knowingly false claims, or abuse the support process.

14. Paid Features Do Not Override Safety Rules

Wallet, KindredPass, Premium, Ready to Meet access, Liked You reveals, Super Likes, and photo comments do not give users permission to violate these Guidelines. Paid access may be restricted, suspended, or revoked where needed to protect users or enforce platform rules.

15. Enforcement

KindredCube may take one or more actions, including content removal, warnings, additional verification, feature restrictions, reduced visibility, temporary suspension, permanent termination, evidence preservation, or cooperation with lawful investigations.

Enforcement decisions may consider severity, frequency, context, verification history, safety reports, post-meet feedback, blocking patterns, payment abuse, and other trust and safety signals. Serious violations may result in immediate permanent removal without prior warning.

16. Appeals

If you believe your account or content was restricted in error, you may submit an appeal through KindredCube Support. Appeals are reviewed individually, but submitting an appeal does not guarantee restoration of an account, feature, or content.

17. Help Us Keep KindredCube Safe

Every honest report, block, verification, support request, post-meet check, and respectful interaction contributes to a safer community.

By using KindredCube, you agree to uphold these Community Guidelines and help foster a platform where meaningful relationships can develop in an environment built on authenticity, respect, and safety.

18. Contact Support

If you need help or want to report a concern, contact support through the app or email support@kindredcube.com.

KindredCube is a values-based dating platform built for deeper compatibility, safer discovery, and more meaningful relationships.
