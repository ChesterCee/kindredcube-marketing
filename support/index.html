Contact KindredCube Support

Support is handled inside the KindredCube mobile app so your account, device, and ticket history stay connected.

How to get help

Open the KindredCube app, go to your Profile, tap the question-mark help button, search the FAQs, then choose Contact Support if you still need help.

When you create a ticket in the app, KindredCube can attach the right account context and keep all replies connected to the same support case.

Why support starts in the app

This protects users, reduces fake requests, and makes sure account-sensitive issues such as payments, verification, profile access, safety reports, and deleted accounts are handled securely.
